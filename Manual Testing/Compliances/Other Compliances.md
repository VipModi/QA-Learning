## 🌎 **Other Major Laws & Standards You Should Know**

### 🔐 **1. LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados)** – Brazil

- 🇧🇷 **Brazil's version of GDPR**
- Covers **all personal data** collected or processed in Brazil
- Gives users rights to access, correct, delete their data
- Requires **explicit consent** for data collection

---

### 🔐 **2. POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act)** – South Africa

- 🇿🇦 South Africa's main data privacy law
- Applies to all organizations processing personal data
- Requires **data minimization, purpose limitation**, and **data subject rights**

---

### 🔐 **3. PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act)** – Canada

- 🇨🇦 Covers how businesses collect, use, and disclose personal info
- Must get **meaningful consent** from users
- Strong **security safeguards** and breach notification rules

---

### 🧠 **4. COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act)** – US

- 🇺🇸 Protects kids **under 13 years old**
- Requires **parental consent** before collecting any data
- Applies to games, websites, and apps directed to children
- Heavy fines if violated (e.g., TikTok was fined millions)

---

### 🧠 **5. FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act)** – US

- 🇺🇸 Protects **student education records**
- Applies to **schools, universities, and EdTech platforms**
- Students have right to access, correct, and restrict data sharing

---

### 🧪 **6. SOC 2 (System and Organization Controls)** – US/Global Standard

- Not a law, but a **security audit framework** used by SaaS and cloud providers
- Covers **data security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy**
- Required by many **enterprise clients** (e.g., in B2B SaaS)

---

### 📚 **7. Section 508 (US)**

- 🇺🇸 US federal accessibility law
- Requires federal websites/software to be **accessible to people with disabilities**
- Based on WCAG standards
- Applies if you're building tech for the US government or receiving federal funds

---

### 🔞 **8. Age-Appropriate Design Code (Children’s Code)** – UK

- 🇬🇧 Designed to **protect children online**
- Requires apps/websites to **default to privacy** for minors
- Applies even if children _might_ use the platform (not just those targeted at them)

---

### 📊 **9. NIST Cybersecurity Framework (US/Global)**

- 🧪 A voluntary set of **best practices** for managing cybersecurity risk
- Used widely in **healthcare, finance, and infrastructure**
- Helps orgs improve **resilience, risk management, and data protection**
