## 🛡️ ISO/IEC 27001 – International Standard for Information Security

📍 **Region:** Global (Published by ISO and IEC)  
🗓️ **First Published:** 2005 (Revised in 2013 and 2022)  
🎯 **Focus:** Establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continuously improving an **Information Security Management System (ISMS)** to **protect data confidentiality, integrity, and availability**.

---

#### 📜 What It Is:

ISO/IEC 27001 is an **internationally recognized framework** that helps organizations **secure sensitive information**—whether digital, paper-based, or spoken. It applies to:
- IT companies
- Financial institutions
- Government bodies
- Any organization managing customer/user data

It’s **voluntary**, but highly recommended for businesses dealing with sensitive or regulated data.

---

#### 🧠 What Does ISO 27001 Protect?

|Data Type|Examples|
|---|---|
|**Confidentiality**|Preventing unauthorized access (e.g., personal user data)|
|**Integrity**|Ensuring data accuracy and trustworthiness (e.g., no unauthorized changes)|
|**Availability**|Making sure systems are up and data is accessible when needed|

---

#### 🔐 ISO 27001 Key Components

|📘 Section|🔍 Description|
|---|---|
|**ISMS Scope**|Defines what information, locations, and systems are covered|
|**Leadership & Policy**|Top management commitment to information security|
|**Risk Assessment**|Identifying, evaluating, and addressing data security risks|
|**Security Controls (Annex A)**|114 controls across 14 categories (access control, encryption, etc.)|
|**Internal Audits & Reviews**|Regular evaluations to ensure ongoing compliance|
|**Continuous Improvement**|Plans for addressing gaps, incidents, and changing risks|

---

#### 🧰 Example Annex A Control Categories:

|Category|Examples of Controls|
|---|---|
|Access Control|Role-based access, MFA, session timeouts|
|Cryptography|Encryption in transit and at rest|
|Operations Security|Antivirus, patch management|
|Incident Response|Incident reporting workflows and logs|
|Compliance|Meeting local laws (like GDPR, HIPAA, etc.)|

---

#### 📲 Does ISO 27001 Apply to Social Media Apps?

Yes—especially if the app handles **user-generated data**, **private messaging**, **location info**, or **personal identifiers**.

🧩 For example:

- Messaging apps (like WhatsApp or Messenger)
    
- Photo-sharing platforms (like Instagram)
    
- Any platform that stores or processes sensitive personal data
    

These platforms should follow ISO 27001 principles for:

- Secure authentication
    
- Data encryption
    
- Access logging
    
- Data breach readiness
    

---

#### 🧪 As a Tester: What to Verify in ISO 27001-Compliant Apps

|🔍 Test Area|✅ What to Check|
|---|---|
|**Authentication**|Strong passwords, MFA enabled|
|**Session Management**|Session expiration, logout after inactivity|
|**Data Encryption**|HTTPS/TLS for data in transit, AES-256 at rest|
|**Access Logs**|Track who accessed what, when|
|**Error Handling**|No sensitive info leaked in error messages|
|**Backups**|Data recovery and backup validation|
|**Risk Mitigation**|Controls in place for high-risk assets or entry points|

---

#### 📋 Example Test Scenario (Secure Login API):

**API:** `POST /auth/login`  
🔧 Payload includes:
- Username
- Password

✅ **Expected:**

- Brute-force protection (e.g., account lockout after failed attempts)
- Password is hashed (not stored in plain text)
- MFA prompt triggered after login
- Login attempt is logged with IP & timestamp
- No stack trace or sensitive data in error response  

---

#### 📉 Impact of Violation:

If a company claims ISO 27001 compliance but **fails to follow its controls**, they risk:

- **Major data breaches**
- **Loss of certifications**
- **Regulatory fines** (especially if tied to GDPR, HIPAA, etc.)
- **Damaged reputation and user trust**
