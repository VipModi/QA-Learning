## 🧒 **COPPA** (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) – U.S. Law

📍 **Region:** United States  
🗓️ **Enacted:** **1998**  
🎯 **Focus:** Protect children's privacy and ensure the safety of personal data for children under **13 years old** in online platforms.

---

#### 📜 **What It Is:**

COPPA is a **U.S. federal law** that regulates the **online collection of personal information** from children under the age of **13**. It aims to protect children from being exploited through data collection, targeted advertising, and unsafe online environments. COPPA ensures that parents have control over the information collected about their children.

---

#### 🔍 **What’s Covered:**

|**Requirement**|**Example in Social Media Apps**|
|---|---|
|Parental Consent|Apps like YouTube must get **explicit consent** from parents before collecting data from users under 13|
|Privacy Policy|Websites/apps must provide clear, **age-appropriate privacy policies** outlining data collection practices|
|Data Collection Limits|**No collection of personal data** from children under 13 without parent consent (e.g., name, address, phone number)|
|User Interaction|If child accounts are allowed, apps must have features to **limit data collection** and **disallow inappropriate content**|
|Third-Party Disclosure|Services cannot share data with third parties without parental consent|

---

#### 🧪 **As a Tester:**

- **Verify Parental Consent Mechanisms:** Test parental consent workflows (e.g., email verification, credit card verification) when creating accounts for users under 13.
- **Check Data Collection:** Ensure that apps do not collect unnecessary personal data from users under 13.
- **Review Privacy Policy:** Ensure the privacy policy is **clear**, **easily accessible**, and **understandable** for parents regarding data collection practices for children.
- **Test Interactive Features:** Verify that features intended for children have **age-appropriate content** and restrictions.

---

#### 🔍 **Key Rules of COPPA:**

- **Parental Consent:** Before collecting personal information from children under 13, apps must obtain **verifiable parental consent**.
- **No Data Sharing:** Platforms cannot **share personal information** collected from children with third parties without parental consent.
- **Privacy Policy:** Platforms must have a **clear privacy policy** detailing their data collection practices and how children's data is handled.
- **Parental Rights:** Parents must be able to **review**, **delete**, or **restrict** the data that has been collected from their children.
- **Age Verification:** If the platform targets children under 13, there must be a way to **verify the user’s age** to ensure they are compliant with COPPA.
    

---

#### 📲 **Impact on Social Media Platforms:**

- **Facebook and Instagram**: Must obtain parental consent before collecting data for users under 13.
- **YouTube**: Must prevent kids' data from being collected and restrict **targeted ads** to child users.
- **TikTok**: Features like **user profiles** and content sharing must have parental consent and proper data safeguards for users under 13.
- **Snapchat**: Must ensure the **age verification process** is robust for users signing up to prevent underage accounts from being created without parental consent.
    

---

#### 📉 **Impact of Violation:**

- **Fines:** Non-compliance can lead to **hefty fines**. The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) can impose penalties of up to **$43,280 per violation**.
- **Reputation Damage:** Significant **loss of trust** from parents and the public, as platforms will be seen as irresponsible in handling children's privacy.
- **Legal Consequences:** Potential for **class-action lawsuits** or other legal actions if children’s data is mishandled.
- **Platform Suspension:** If found violating COPPA, platforms may face **legal injunctions** or forced to halt operations or make changes in user services.
