#agile #userstory
## 📘 What is a **User Story**?

A **User Story** is a **short, simple description** of a feature told from the perspective of the **end-user or customer**.

It answers:
- Who needs it?
- What do they need?
- Why do they need it?

---

## ✍️ Format of a User Story:

> **As a** `<type of user>`  
> **I want** `<some goal or action>`  
> **So that** `<some reason or benefit>`

---

### ✅ Example of a Good User Story:

> **As a** user  
> **I want** to be able to delete a message for everyone in a chat  
> **So that** I can remove accidentally sent content before it's seen.

---

## 📄 What Document Should It Be Part Of?

User Stories are part of the **Product Backlog** – which is maintained by the **Product Owner** in Agile.

- Each story is usually documented in tools like **JIRA**, **Azure DevOps**, **ClickUp**, or **Trello**.    
- They can also be in a **Product Requirements Document (PRD)** or a **User Story document** shared with QA and dev teams.
- In some teams, stories are also included in **Sprint Backlog** once pulled for a sprint.
    

---

## 🎯 What Should Be Included with a User Story?

1. **User Story Statement** (as shown above)
2. **Acceptance Criteria** – Conditions to consider the story done
3. **Priority / Estimate** – Usually in story points
4. **Attachments** – Screens, design mocks, links, etc.
5. **Tags** – For features, platforms (iOS/Android), etc.
    

---

### 🧪 Example with Acceptance Criteria:

#### 🧾 User Story:

> **As a** user  
> **I want** to hide my online status  
> **So that** I can chat without others knowing I’m online.

#### ✅ Acceptance Criteria:

- User can turn off online status in Settings.
- When online status is off, others can’t see it.
- Works on both Android and iOS.
- Changes reflect instantly across devices.
    

---

## 📌 Bonus: How Testers Use User Stories

As a QA, you:

- **Review stories** before the sprint (in grooming sessions).
- **Clarify** testable points with the Product Owner.
- **Write test cases** based on the story and acceptance criteria.
- **Validate** the story is working as expected.
